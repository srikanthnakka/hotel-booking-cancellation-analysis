# Hotel Booking Cancellation Analysis

##  Project Overview
This project analyzes hotel booking cancellations to identify key drivers of cancellations and quantify their revenue impact. The analysis is performed using Microsoft Excel with pivot tables, charts, slicers, and an interactive dashboard.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze overall cancellation rate
- Identify high-risk booking patterns
- Measure revenue loss due to cancellations
- Understand seasonal and yearly trends
- Provide business recommendations to reduce revenue leakage

---

##  Key Insights
- Overall cancellation rate is ~37%
- Long lead-time bookings have higher cancellation rates
- Late cancellations contribute disproportionately to revenue loss
- Online Travel Agencies (OTA) generate the highest revenue loss
- Peak-season months (April–August) show the highest cancellation impact
- Revenue loss increased significantly after 2015 and remained high

---

##  Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
- Slicers for interactivity
- KPI metrics
- Dashboard design
- Business-oriented analysis

---

## Dashboard Features
- KPI summary (Total Bookings, Cancellation Rate, Revenue Lost, Late Cancellation %)
- Cancellation analysis by lead time, customer type, and booking channel
- Month-wise and year-wise trend analysis
- Interactive slicers for dynamic exploration

##  Conclusion
The analysis shows that cancellations are driven by lead time, booking channel, and seasonality. Applying risk-based and seasonal cancellation policies can significantly reduce revenue loss.
